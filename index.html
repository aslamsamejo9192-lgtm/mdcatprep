About MdcatPrep

MdcatPrep

MdcatPrep is an online practice platform built for students preparing for medical and dental college entry tests in Pakistan. It brings chapter-wise practice for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English and Logical Reasoning together in one simple place, so you can prepare with a clear routine instead of scattered material.

Chapter-Wise Practice

Preparation is organised chapter by chapter, so you can focus on one topic at a time and build your understanding step by step.

Mock Tests

Complete exam-style papers that bring questions from every subject together, so you can sit a full practice exam in one go and review your performance afterwards.

Instant Results & Review

As soon as you finish a test, you can see your result and review every question to learn from your mistakes.

Made for Students

Everything here is designed around a student's routine — simple to use, easy to follow, and centred on steady, focused preparation.
