About MdcatPrep

MdcatPrep is an MDCAT preparation platform built for Pakistani students aiming for medical and dental college admission. It brings the complete PMDC syllabus together in one place — chapter-wise tests, detailed notes and structured revision material for all five subjects.

Instead of scattered material, you prepare chapter by chapter: read the notes, attempt the timed test for that chapter, review your result, and move on. That is how toppers revise — and it is the routine MdcatPrep is designed around.

  • 54 chapter-wise tests with 2,700 MCQs across Biology, Chemistry and Physics
  • 62 detailed chapter notes covering all five PMDC subjects
  • Timed tests with result and per-question review
  • Content aligned with the PMDC syllabus and FSc pre-medical coursework
